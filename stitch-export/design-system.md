## Retrieved Guidelines

The Stitch design system describes a modern SaaS planner for high-velocity social media teams. It uses a sidebar-driven dashboard layout, a 12-column grid, an 8px spacing rhythm, white cards on a soft slate/lavender background, subtle borders and shadows, purple primary buttons, emerald success states, amber warning states, high-density Kanban cards, metric cards, data tables, and compact calendar widgets.

## Critique

The system intent is strong, but the generated screens interpret it too literally and too separately. Purple and pale lavender become the dominant experience instead of accents. The repeated exports also create design drift: radius, header density, active nav placement, and dashboard rhythm vary from page to page. The improved local prototype keeps the strategic planner identity but makes the palette more neutral, the screens more operational, and the components more reusable.
